Privacy Policy — SematIn

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SematIn is a browser extension for saving reusable text templates and filling them into web pages. This policy explains what data SematIn handles and where it goes.

Your templates stay in your browser

Your templates, categories, and settings are stored locally in your browser (Chrome storage). They are never sent to us. SematIn has no account system, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

Images you insert

When you insert a picture into a template, SematIn uploads that image to Catbox (catbox.moe) to get a shareable link, because web pages can only display images that have a URL. Only the images you choose to insert are uploaded, and only at the moment you insert them. Images already stored in your library are reused without re-uploading.

Template import from a URL

If you import templates from a URL, SematIn fetches that URL — the address you entered — to download the template file. Nothing is fetched unless you ask for it.

What we never do

SematIn does not collect, sell, or share personal data. It does not read the content of pages you visit except for the text field you are filling when you use a template.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
